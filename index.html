About the Mod

Enormous.
Authentic.
Legendary.

This mod has been in development since LostsouL started the project for NFL 2K5 in March of 2021. We continue to build off of what the team and community has put together. This marks 20+ years of NFL 2K5 Football - and what better way to celebrate this legend than with another updated texture mod to continue the authenticity that we know players will appreciate and enjoy.

🏟️
Stadiums

Community-contributed stadium textures from BigTimeEmpire and MisterAlex bring every venue to life. All 32 NFL stadiums rebuilt with modern accuracy.

👕
Uniforms

Every uniform updated for the 2025–26 season. Alternate kits, color rush, throwbacks - all 32 teams covered with pixel-perfect accuracy.

📋
Complete Rosters

2025–26 NFL rosters with Madden player portraits, playbooks on memcard slot 2, and full franchise file. BAD_AL's automation keeps them sharp all season.

🎨
UI Overhaul

Menus and branding rebuilt from the ground up for the 2025–26 season. Feels native to the era, refined for today's players.

📦
All-In-One Installer

Classic Mod, Super Bowl Mod, UFL Mod, and every uniform GME included. One install gets you everything - ready to play immediately.

📺
Berman Highlights

Video halftime highlights with the legendary Chris Berman experience. Requires the 40GB vhdd file to be created and formatted.

🌐
Community Driven

New GME mods with the "_25C" suffix are community-provided updates. This mod belongs to everyone who loves football.
